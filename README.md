I'm a **PhD researcher in Computer Engineering** at the **Universidad de Sevilla**, affiliated with **CABIMER**.

🧬 My thesis focuses on computational approaches to women's health during hormonal transitions, as part of the [**HormoBiome**](https://www.hormobiome.com/) research group.<br>
🌍 I collaborate with an international consortium: Charité Berlin, HTWG Konstanz and Reutlingen University.<br>
🎓 Health Engineering (BSc) and Software Engineering: Cloud, Data & IT Management (MSc), both at the Universidad de Sevilla.

## 🔬 Research lines

- **Sleep & polysomnography**: microarchitecture biomarkers beyond conventional PSG scoring
- **Microbiome & estrobolome**: β-glucuronidase (GUS) profiling, 16S and metagenomics
- **Women's health**: endometriosis, Sjögren's syndrome and confounding factors in clinical cohorts
- **Health informatics & ML**: interpretable machine learning for clinical data
